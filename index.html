Weiss Privacy & Consent

To offer you a more personal experience, Weiss uses the Connected tier by default. This shares relevant signals with your advisor and Porsche North America to enhance your journey. By continuing, you consent to this level of connection. For more information, please read our Privacy Notice.

Weiss · Privacy & consent

Choose how Weiss knows you

This setting affects what Weiss retains, learns, and shares. It does not affect any purchase decision and can be adjusted anytime.

Standard

A knowledgeable guide

  • Answers every question about your car and the Porsche world.
  • Each session starts fresh; no long-term memory.
  • Nothing is shared beyond this conversation.

Personalized

A companion that remembers

  • Remembers what you explore and what draws your attention over time.
  • Conversations continue where they left off; preferences are stored.
  • Everything it learns stays between you and Weiss.

Connected

A relationship across Porsche

  • Works alongside your dealer and Porsche North America.
  • Shares relevant signals with your advisor so they know where you are in your journey.
  • Includes a full transparency log: you can see the exact content shared.

You can change this from settings at any time. Changes take effect immediately.
